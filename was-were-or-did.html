Past Simple · A2–B1

Was, wereor did?

Sometimes the story needs «to be». Sometimes it needs an action verb with «did». Let’s learn to tell them apart.

Practice · 6 exercises
the rule

One quick question to ask yourself

Задай себе один вопрос — и выбор станет очевидным.

1. Is there an action in the sentence?
play, watch, go, buy, study, eat, have, like… (a verb you can “do”)
2. Yes → use did + the base verb.
Did you watch it? · What did she buy?
3. No (state, place, feeling, age, description) → use was / were.
Were you tired? · Where was he?

was / were

«быть»: где? какой? кто? сколько лет?

  • Was she at school?
  • Where were you born?
  • It wasn’t cold.
  • Yes, I was. / No, they weren’t.

did + verb

действие: делал, смотрел, купил…

  • Did she study?
  • What did you eat?
  • I didn’t like it.
  • Yes, I did. / No, they didn’t.
Never mix them!   Did you were late? → Were you late?  ·  Did he went? → Did he go?
В вопросе с did глагол всегда в начальной форме. «Was/were» уже сам «помощник» — did рядом не нужен.
